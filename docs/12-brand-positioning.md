# 12. Brand Positioning — the honour frame

> Research response to Rob's brief: *"honour based, pride centralised, inner self looking and
> motivational to those who seek good change and want to push themselves beyond what they
> believed to be their limits."* Written 23 Aug 2026. Decisions at the end are Rob's.

---

## 12.1 The tension, resolved

**They are not in conflict. But the current brand is not actually austere — it is
contemptuous, and that is the part that has to go.**

Austerity is *unadorned*. Contempt is a *posture toward the reader*. The copy has both, and
only one is an asset.

**Austere, keep:** "Fifty-seven stages. One line at a time. No skipping ahead." · "The
halfway tee. Earned, not bought." *(the best sentence on the site)*

**Contemptuous, cut:** "The board doesn't care how you feel about it." · "Most never see it."
· "No exceptions. No shortcuts." · "YOU'RE NOT ON THE START LINE YET."

Those four lines are the entire source of the tension. They are not cold, they are faintly
hostile — the brand talking down to someone who has just handed over money. Remove the
hostility and austerity and honour stop conflicting, because **every honour tradition is
austere.** The Iron Ring obligation is one unadorned sentence. A judo grading certificate has
no adjectives on it. Gravity is the native register of honour; cheerfulness is not.

### The register: a registrar of an order

Not a coach, not a drill sergeant, not a race announcer — **a keeper of a record.** Formal,
exact, unhurried, entirely without encouragement, and nonetheless deeply respectful, because
the respect is carried by the precision.

| Rob asked for | Delivered by | Not by |
|---|---|---|
| Honour-based | The Undertaking, and the fact that you verify nothing | Saying the word "honour" |
| Pride-centralised | Conferral — scarce, witnessed, physical | Badges, points, praise |
| Inner-self-looking | The cooldown, reframed as *the interval* | "Reflect on your why" prompts |
| Motivational | Sequence, witness, record | Exhortation |

**What must be given up: exhortation, in both directions.** No "crush it", and equally no "the
board doesn't care". The house never tells a member how to feel, and never tells them what
they are not.

### The insight the brand should be built on

The disclaimer already says it: *we do not verify what you actually did.* The button says
MARK CLEARED. No proof, no photo, no GPS, no witness.

**That is not a gap in the product. That is the product.** The 57 Trials is a self-attested
order. The only thing making a cleared trial mean anything is a member's own word, kept when
nobody is looking.

Honour is therefore not a warm layer retro-fitted onto a cold product — it is **the
load-bearing mechanism already shipped and never named.** And austerity is what makes it
credible: a brand that cheered you on would be a brand it costs nothing to lie to.

> **Nobody checks. That is the whole of it.**

## 12.2 Mechanisms worth borrowing

Audited against judo/karate grading, the Iron Ring, livery companies, the Duke of Edinburgh's
Award, parkrun milestones, and mountaineering ethics.

**Adopt — authentic to what exists:**
1. **The obligation, not the promise.** Kipling's Iron Ring text binds the engineer *not to pass bad workmanship* — negative, specific, modest, promising no greatness. It also sidesteps every advertising-claims problem.
2. **Witnesses.** An oath with no witness is a diary entry. **The biggest missing mechanism.**
3. **Fixed progressive rank in non-negotiable order.** Already enforced in the database. This is structurally a grading system that hasn't admitted it.
4. **Minimum time in grade.** Every serious belt system has one. `min_gap_minutes` *is* this — reframe it from a lockout into a requirement.
5. **Scarce physical conferral.** Already right. "Earned, not bought" is already the correct sentence.
6. **The right to confer, earned by completing.** The highest-status mechanism in every tradition, and it costs nothing.
7. **A published, unchanging code.** Climbing's whole integrity system is self-reported honour against a published standard.
8. **A register.** The record *is* the institution.
9. **Rank confers duty, not privilege.** This is what stops honour becoming caste.

**Reject — would read as borrowed or hollow:** military rank language (test: would a serving
soldier find it embarrassing?) · secrecy about requirements · invented honorifics · staged
digital "ceremony" · Latin and heraldry · explaining the anthropology.

## 12.3 Positioning

**The axes that discriminate:** where the standard comes from (self-set ↔ externally fixed),
and what completion produces (private feeling ↔ durable conferred status).

- **75 Hard** — maximally fixed, produces almost nothing durable. A million claimed completions and **no roll of names.**
- **Finch** — fully self-set, private, warm. ~$30M ARR. That market is owned; don't chase it.
- **The Conqueror** — fixed-ish, posts a medal, but a **91.4% completion rate** means the medal confers little.
- **parkrun** — fixed format, permanent barcode, permanent results, milestone shirts. **This is the real reference brand, not 75 Hard.** Note its vocabulary is civil-registry, and it insists it is *not a race.*
- **The 57 Trials** — the far corner on both axes, and nobody is there.

**The third dimension nobody occupies: shape of commitment.** Five to nine months is too long
to be a challenge and too finite to be a membership. That is exactly the shape of the Duke of
Edinburgh's Award — **572,802 UK participants in 2024–25**, a record year. The shape has proven
mass UK appeal. **Nobody sells it to adults.**

### Positioning statement
> For adults in the UK who want to change something and are repelled by the way that is
> usually sold to them, **The 57 Trials is a fixed order of fifty-seven trials, taken strictly
> in sequence over five to nine months.** Unlike challenge programmes you can restart, adapt or
> buy your way past, the order is enforced, the intervals are compulsory, and nothing you do is
> ever checked — the record stands on your word. It ends in a number that is yours permanently
> and can never be issued to anyone else.

### Brand promise
> **Fifty-seven trials, in order, on your word.**

**The moat is a register that appreciates.** Bib 58 becomes more meaningful as the register
lengthens. Permanence cannot be launched, only accrued. That is the answer to "75 Hard is
free" — 75 Hard is free and gives you nothing that persists.

## 12.4 Voice

**Ten rules:** state facts and consequences, never judgements about the member · **never say
what a member is not** · warmth is precision of memory, never praise · every lock names what
opens it and when · plain Anglo-Saxon verbs (*keep, sign, hold, stand, open, close*) · no
exclamation marks, no emoji, no superlatives · banned: *crush, smash, unlock your potential,
journey, transform, no excuses, mindset, grind, elite* · never imply an outcome (voice rule
and CAP Code rule at once) · language rises **only** at conferral · number in formal contexts,
name in personal ones.

**The test before shipping any string:** *Would a registrar say this? Would a race marshal say
this? Would a motivational coach say this?* Ship only on yes, yes, no.

**The most important new string,** shown after every signature — and it converts the most
irritating constraint into the most distinctive asset:

> Trial 22 is signed. Trial 23 opens in 24 hours.
> **The interval is part of the trial.**

### The Undertaking
Shown once, at entry, before payment. First person, specific, negative, promising nothing:

> I enter as Bib No. ____.
>
> I will take the fifty-seven in the order they are given.
> I will sign for nothing I have not done.
> I will not ask for a trial to be opened early.
> If I stop, I will say so plainly.
>
> Nobody will check. That is the whole of it.

Then one field: **Witness (optional).** A name and email of someone who is not a member. They
receive two emails ever — the undertaking at entry, and the completed record at 57. That is an
oath with a witness, and quietly it is also the only zero-cost acquisition channel in the plan.

## 12.5 Keep / change / add

**Keep:** the name · 57 · permanent never-reissued bib numbers · `SERIAL NO. 5757-####` ·
Bebas Neue + IBM Plex Mono · the palette · DB-enforced sequence · the intervals · physical
drops · "Earned, not bought" · the route visualiser · the terseness.

**Change:** the four contemptuous strings · MARK CLEARED → SIGN FOR IT · Circuit Pass →
Standing · remove confetti, the padlock emoji and the exclamation mark · retire "line" as a
synonym for trial · *(judgement call)* demote yellow from primary CTA to state colour.

**Retire the ranked leaderboard.** Nothing is verified and the intervals cap the maximum
honest rate, so a competitive board rewards exactly one behaviour: signing for things you
didn't do. **A ranked board is the most anti-honour object in the product.** Replace with
**the Register** — every member in entry-number order, no ranking. parkrun publishes results
and milestones and is emphatic that it is not a race.

**Add:** the Undertaking and the Witness · **the right to stand as witness**, earned by
completing (*"Bib 0142, witnessed by Bib 0061"* — gives finishers a role after finishing,
which is the retention problem, and builds a lineage graph nobody else has) · **the Code**,
five to seven published lines, versioned, never retroactive · a better 57 artefact — small,
plain, worn constantly, legible only to those who know, rather than a framed poster.

## 12.6 The metaphor, the lore, the name

**Layer the race under the register, don't replace it.** Right now race is the ground and
honour is absent; it should be the reverse. Keep bib, number, route, checkpoint, mark, stamp.
Retire timing tent, board, standings, "climb", "line", "runner", and anything implying
competition between members.

Three reasons: the mechanics are *already* a grading system, not a race, and the race metaphor
is actively fighting them (which is why the leaderboard sorts in a way that rewards lying) ·
the metaphor picks the competitive set, and race framing puts you next to free 75 Hard while
register framing puts you next to DofE and parkrun milestones, where nobody sells to adults ·
**the bib survives intact**, because a race bib already *is* a register mark.

**5757 — leave unexplained.** One piece of unexplained lore is texture; two is a gimmick.

**Bibs 001–057 — confer them.** Never issued, not for sale, held for the **first fifty-seven
people to complete all fifty-seven trials**, in finishing order. `Bib 0058 · Founding 003`. No
conflict with never-reissued, and it gives the first cohort a permanent visible reason to
finish — which is the real pre-launch problem.

**Retire the Primes motif.** *This is the researcher's most direct disagreement with the
current brand, and it is a good argument.* The Grothendieck joke is about **a number that
looks prime but isn't** — something that appears genuine and fails inspection. For a brand
whose whole proposition is that the record can be trusted, that is precisely the wrong
resonance, and anyone who gets the reference gets that reading too. The on-brand answer to
"why 57?" is: **because that is how many there are.**

**The name: keep it, file it.** "Trial" carries ordeal, test of worth, **and legal proceeding**
— hearing, testimony, judgment, record. That third sense is a gift to an honour brand and
nobody in the category uses it. It also promises no outcome, which makes it the safest
possible name under the CAP Code.

## 12.7 Audience

Austere-only selects for people who already identify as disciplined: largely men 18–35 already
orbiting Frisella — the most contested, least monetisable slice, where the incumbent is free.

Honour/rank/permanent-record widens toward people motivated by **belonging and a legible
record**. Evidence: DofE's 572,802 participants, gender-balanced and mass · parkrun at
56/39 male/female with the gap narrowing, milestone shirts as its central retention device ·
Strava's women >40% of new athletes and run-club joins up 89% · and Sport England finding
**51% of lower-income women say they don't belong in gyms** — *belonging* is the named barrier,
which an honour frame addresses directly and "no excuses" makes worse.

**The widening is not "women" as a demographic.** It is people who want a serious commitment
with a recognised standard and no shouting — roughly 28–45, mixed gender, typically already
doing one structured thing.

**The beachhead: people whose lives already run on codes and gradings.** Martial arts club
members. Scout and Guide leaders, DofE Gold holders. Reservists and cadet force adults.
Nurses, engineers, teachers, trades apprentices. They already believe an oath means something
and already accept that rank takes time. **Recruit the first fifty-seven from there, by hand,
by name.** The Discord failed because a Discord asks people to join nothing in particular; an
undertaking asks them to join something specific.

## 12.8 Failure modes and guards

| Failure | Trigger | Guard |
|---|---|---|
| **Quasi-military cosplay** | Borrowed rank names, "operator", "STAND DOWN" | Civil-register vocabulary only. Test: would a serving soldier find this embarrassing? |
| **Contempt / toxic masculinity** | The four strings above; any shame mechanic | *Never say what a member is not.* No body-composition talk. Market the intervals as anti-grind — a real, defensible split from 75 Hard's shame loop |
| **Cult aesthetics** | Unexplained authority, secret requirements, anonymous "we", isolation | Rob is named and visible · every requirement published in advance · rules versioned, never retroactive · leaving is one click and the copy says so · **the Witness points outward to non-members**, the strongest anti-cult design there is |
| **Exclusionary caste** | Founding numbers reading as an elite | Earned by finishing, open to anyone · register lists everyone equally · **rank confers duty (standing as witness), never privilege** |
| **Hollow badges** | Conferring often, cheaply, or digitally | Four physical drops and the finisher mark. **No streaks, no points, no XP, no digital badges.** |
| **Farming the honour system** | A ranked board on unverified data | Retire the ranked board. Register by entry number. |
| **Regulatory** | Outcome claims, testimonials | No transformation or health claims. Get the "DRAFT — NOT REVIEWED BY A SOLICITOR" banner resolved before launch. Design for the DMCCA subscription regime now. |

## 12.9 Decisions for Rob

1. **Founding numbers 001–057 conferred on completion, or permanently unexplained?** Recommendation: conferred.
2. **Retire the ranked leaderboard for a register?** Recommendation: yes, strongly, and before launch rather than after people are on it.
3. **Demote yellow from primary CTA to state colour?** Judgement call; researcher leans yes.
4. **Homepage line — "No skipping ahead" or "Nobody checks. That is the whole of it."?** The second is braver and more ownable, and could be misread by a casual reader as an admission of weakness.
5. **"Standing" for the monthly.** Clearly right — the guild word, accurate, and *"your standing lapses; your record does not"* is both true and legally clean. Unusual for a subscription; needs one line of explanation.
6. **Retire the Primes motif?** The argument against it is strong. Rob's call, since the lore is his.
