**THE LINE**

Ten small promises made to yourself at dawn, checked against what actually happened by
night. A day-long version of the pledge from Trial 03.

**THE WORK**

1. First thing in the morning, write down ten small promises for the day — specific, achievable things, not vague intentions. "Reply to that email," not "be more on top of things."
2. Keep them realistic. Ten promises you're confident you can keep is a better trial than ten aspirational ones you're likely to break.
3. Go about your day. Don't check the list obsessively, but don't forget it either.
4. At night, go back through all ten and mark honestly which were kept and which weren't.
5. Write one honest line about any that weren't — not an excuse, just what actually got in the way.

**THE RUBRIC**

*RISK* — Writing promises vague enough to claim as kept regardless of what happens, which
defeats the trial entirely. Specific and checkable is the whole exercise.

*GROUND* — Nothing physical, just the ordinary shape of a day you'd have anyway.

*STOP* — If the day derails entirely for reasons outside your control, that's real
information for the evening review, not a reason to abandon the trial.

*SCALE* — Five promises instead of ten, at the same standard of specific and checkable.

*CLEARED WHEN* — Ten promises, or five scaled, are written at dawn and honestly checked off
at night.

**NOTE**

Most people keep more of these than they expect to, and are more surprised by which ones
they broke.
