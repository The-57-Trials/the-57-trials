**THE LINE**

A session finished before the sky is properly light. Not because early is virtuous — because
it changes what the session feels like.

**THE WORK**

1. Pick any movement session you've already done here — the Stack, a walk, whatever suits.
2. Do it, and finish it, before sunrise for your location on the day you choose.
3. Check sunrise time the night before and work backwards from it, so you're not guessing in the dark about how long you have.
4. Lights on, high-visibility if you're outdoors near traffic — dark doesn't mean unlit.
5. Same pace and effort as you'd use for that session any other time of day. Early isn't an excuse to go harder or softer.

**THE RUBRIC**

*RISK* — Poor visibility, for you and to traffic if you're outdoors near roads.
Misjudging sunrise time and finishing in full daylight, which doesn't clear the trial.

*GROUND* — If outdoors: a route you know well enough to walk in low light, high-visibility
or a light if there's any traffic, phone charged. Indoors is completely valid if outdoors in
the dark doesn't feel safe.

*STOP* — Stop for the day if any of these happen: chest pain or tightness · breathlessness
that doesn't settle within a minute of stopping · dizziness that doesn't clear when you sit
down · any sharp or new pain. Also turn back and try indoors instead if the ground or
lighting doesn't feel safe once you're out there.

*SCALE* — Choose an indoor session if outdoor darkness doesn't feel safe · a shorter version
of whichever session you picked, provided it still finishes before sunrise.

*CLEARED WHEN* — The session is finished before sunrise, logged the same day.

**NOTE**

Almost nobody else is awake for this one. That's most of what makes it feel different.
