# 7–8. Growth, and Operations

> Status: draft for Rob's review. Written 22 Aug 2026. Costs researched, sources at the end.

---

## 7.0 The number that should shape everything below

Run the unit economics first, because they change the marketing answer.

A member who **completes** all 57 pays £10 + ~8 months × £4.99 = **£49.92 gross, ~£47.40
net** after Stripe. Four posted milestone parcels cost **£8–14 each** all-in once you count
item, mailer and Royal Mail (2nd class small parcel floor £4.39). That's **£32–44 of cost on
your best customers.**

> ### At current pricing, the business only makes money on people who quit.

That is structurally bad, and it constrains marketing: you cannot afford paid acquisition,
and you cannot afford to be relaxed about the merch spec.

**Two fixes. Take both.**

| Lever | Change | Effect on a completer |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Circuit Pass £4.99 → **£7.99/mo** | Net ~£47 → ~£71 |
| Merch | 15/30/45 become **Large Letter items** (~£4 all-in each); only 57 is substantial (~£16) | Cost ~£38 → ~£28 |

Together a completer is worth **~£43 of margin instead of ~£6**. £7.99 is defensible — 57
briefings over eight months with posted merch is not a £4.99 product, and £4.99 signals
"cheap app" against an austere premium brand.

**Test £7.99 from day one.** You have zero customers; there is no legacy pricing to break.
Cheapest change in the plan, largest effect.

## 7.1 Acquisition channels, ranked

**1. Organic short-form video, founder-fronted.** £0 cash, 6–10 hrs/week. The only channel
with the right shape. **70% of marketing hours go here.**

**2. The residual Discord audience.** £0, 1–2 hrs to test, and **the highest-value unknown in
the plan.** A warm list converts at 15–30% landing→signup versus 2–5% cold. Do this in week
one of pre-launch, before anything else: one message, no pitch, a UTM-tagged bib-reservation
link. **Under 10 clicks in 48 hours = treat the audience as zero and never plan around it.**

**3. Reddit and niche communities.** £0, 2–3 hrs/week, mostly not posting. A 2026 study of 49
subreddits founders pitch in found **39% ban self-promotion outright and 22% more allow it
only under a 9:1 rule**. One good "I built this" post in the subs that permit it is a
one-time spike of 500–3,000 visitors. Also free research: the language people use about 75
Hard and "I need structure". Don't build a growth plan on it.

**4. Creator partnerships — gifted, never paid.** UK micro-influencers charge **£250–£1,000
per Instagram post, £300–£1,200 per TikTok**. At £10 entry, a £500 post needs 50+
conversions. Not viable. **Gifted is:** offer 10–20 small creators a **reserved low bib
number (0006–0030)** plus free Circuit Pass. The scarcity is the payment, and unpaid posts
are more credible. Start day ~45.

**5. Paid ads — ignore for 90 days.** UK TikTok CPCs £0.30–£1.50. At £0.80/click, 3%
landing→signup, 25% signup→paid:
> £0.80 ÷ (0.03 × 0.25) = **£107 to acquire a customer who pays you £9.65 today.**

**6. PR — ignore entirely.** No story yet. Bank two future angles: the founder completing all
57, and the lore/ARG angle.

**Concentrate: 70% video, 15% community, 15% creator seeding from day 45. Zero on ads and PR.**

## 7.2 The content problem

**Be honest: "person does a physical challenge" is completely undifferentiated in 2026.**
#75hardchallenge has passed a billion TikTok views, and 2026 has already produced 75 Soft
and 75 Medium. If your content is "watch me do trial 12", you lose.

**What is differentiated is the apparatus** — a race organisation with no race. Bibs, timing
boards, sealed briefings, permanent numbered identity, unexplained lore. Nobody else in this
category is austere. **Lead with the system; the physical act is B-roll.**

### Shareable artifacts, ranked
| Artifact | Acquisition | Retention | Build cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bib number** (permanent, low ones scarce) | **Highest** | High | Low |
| **CLEARED stamp** | High | High | Low |
| **Redacted briefing** | High | Medium | Zero — it's the paywall, photographed |
| Route visualiser filling | Low early, high later | **Highest** | Built; needs export |
| Merch unboxing | Medium (arrives late) | High | Planned |
| Leaderboard | Low — exposes small numbers | Medium | Hide totals |

**Critical build:** the CLEARED stamp and bib card must be **app-generated images, not
screenshots**. A screenshot of a dashboard is bad content; a designed, dated, bib-numbered
share card with your URL on it is free advertising. **Highest-ROI development work remaining.**

### Five formats that would get watched
1. **The redacted briefing** — cold open on a briefing card mostly blacked out, voice reads only the constraint, cut to attempt. *The paywall becomes the hook.* Two of every three posts.
2. **Failures, not successes.** Better watch-through, more comments, and it makes the difficulty credible — which is what sells the entry.
3. **System-voice bulletins.** *"Bib 0043 has cleared 12. Bib 0007 has not moved in nine days."* Text on black, terse VO. Ten minutes to make, no filming, turns members into content. **The only format that scales without founder time.**
4. **The lore.** 5757, the Primes. Over-indexes on saves and shares. **One condition: there must be an actual answer.** Decide what 5757 means and write it down.
5. **"Reading the rules."** Straight to camera, no commentary. The austerity is the content.

**Never make:** day-in-the-life vlogs, "day 1 of 57", transformations, motivational voiceover
over gym footage.

**Cadence: 5 posts/week minimum for 90 days**, same asset across all three platforms. Below
4/week you cannot learn what works. Batch-film one weekend day.

## 7.3 Launch plan (8–12 hrs/week around a day job)

### Pre-launch, weeks −6 to −1 (~60 hrs) — hard gates
| # | Item | Hrs | Why it's a gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trials 01–20 written** (30 preferred) | 20–30 | Launching with 5 is a fire, not a launch |
| 2 | Bundle £10 to include 30 days Circuit Pass | 3 | Biggest conversion lever available |
| 3 | CLEARED + bib card generators | 4–6 | Your only free distribution |
| 4 | Cooldown timers | 2 | Content survival + brand-consistent |
| 5 | Injury alias, 4h SLA, auto-freeze | 3 | Non-negotiable for a physical product |
| 6 | Insurance covering **online/remote delivery** | 3 | UK Coaching **£105/yr** for £10m PL incl. online; bundled PL+PI **£80–180/yr** |
| 7 | Away-mode flag + 5-page runbook | 4 | §8.5 |
| 8 | Waitlist with **bib reservation, cap 0001–0100** | 2 | The launch mechanic |

**In parallel from week −6:** post 3×/week. Do not launch cold into empty accounts.

### Launch week (~15 hrs)
Open bibs 0001–0100. Hard cap, visible counter, permanent "FOUNDING" mark. **The scarcity is
the entire pitch** — not features. *"One hundred numbers. Then the field closes for the first
circuit."* One post per day, all pointing at scarcity. **Email every single member personally
with one question: "What made you pay?"** At 100 members that's 2 hours and the best
positioning research you'll ever get.

### Days 8–30 · 31–60 · 61–90
Fix the funnel on real data, write to trial 30. Then creator seeding, first merch batch,
Discord only if 50+ active. Then first retention data, and only now a £150–300 paid test.
**Build no new features.**

### Tripwires, not forecasts
Cold start: **30–80 Entry Passes in 90 days.** With a real Discord audience: **100–250.**
> **If under 20 Entry Passes at day 60 while genuinely shipping 5 posts/week, the problem is
> the offer or the channel, not effort. Change something structural. Do not work harder.**

## 7.4 The funnel, and the trial-06 problem

| Step | Expected | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Landing → signup | 2–5% cold / **15–30% warm** | Why the Discord test matters so much |
| Signup → £10 | 10–20% cold / 30–50% warm | Consent gates add real friction before payment |
| £10 → clears trial 01 | 70–85% | Some pay and never start |
| Trial 01 → 05 | 45–60% | Largely healthy attrition |
| **Trial 05 → £4.99** | **20–35%** | **Where the business dies** |
| Monthly retention after | 88–92% | Fitness apps average **9.2% monthly churn** |

Cold, end to end: **1,000 visitors → ~30 signups → ~6 Entry Passes → ~2 subscribers.**

### Three things are wrong with the trial-06 wall, and only one is the number 5
1. **It's a second *purchase* decision, not a *renewal* decision.** These convert very differently.
2. **At 5 of 57 the route visualiser is 8.8% full.** The member doesn't see momentum — they see 52 empty checkpoints and a bill. Your best retention asset is working against you at the paywall.
3. **The wall is blank.** A locked door with the shape of the room visible converts far better than a blank one.

### Recommendation — change the packaging, not the number
> **The £10 Entry Pass includes the first 30 days of Circuit Pass, auto-renewing at £7.99/month.**

One checkout, full access from minute one, and the day-30 decision becomes *"do I cancel?"*
rather than *"do I buy again?"* At day 30 with cooldowns the member has cleared ~12–18
trials, the route is ~25% full, they have a streak, a rank, and merch at 15 in touching
distance. Expect **55–70% month-1 retention** instead of 20–35% conversion. It also makes the
£10 read as a bargain rather than a deposit, improving the *first* conversion too.

Make the renewal loud and honest — an austere brand cannot afford to look like it hid a
subscription. On the button: *"£10 today. Circuit Pass £7.99/month from day 31. Cancel any
time."* Reminder at day 24.

**Also:** show checkpoints 01–15 only at low progress, with the rest collapsed. Reveal trial
06's number, title and first line, then black-bar the rest — redaction converts, blankness
doesn't. Mention the milestone-15 merch at trial 03.

**Add an annual option at day 30: £69/year** (~£5.75/mo). The product has a natural ~8-month
arc; monthly billing on a finite product invites churn at the end.

## 7.5 Referral and community

**Bib scarcity as referral currency.** Never discount a £10 product. Refer someone who pays,
and you both get something **numbered and non-monetary** — an extra hand-numbered patch, a
marked bib, first access to a drop. Scarcity costs £2; a discount cheapens the brand.

**The Pacer mechanic — build this.** Two members pair up; both must clear the same trial
within 48 hours or the pair breaks. Simultaneously the strongest retention feature available
(paired accountability beats any notification) and inherently viral (you cannot pair without
recruiting). And a race has pacers.

**Timed drops** for trials 45–57 on fixed dates — everyone hits them together.

**Discord: yes, as community only, never as the product.** The original failure was
structural, not social. Hard boundaries: the app owns payment, gating, progress and identity;
Discord owns conversation, and holds no content ever. Roles granted automatically via OAuth.
Channels banded by checkpoint so nobody sees spoilers. A read-only `#results` channel the app
posts into — self-populating social proof, zero effort.

**Do not open below ~50 active members.** An empty Discord is public evidence nobody is here.
Below that, a **weekly Race Bulletin email** does the same job at a tenth of the moderation
cost — and may be the better long-term answer for a solo operator anyway.

# 8. Operations

## 8.1 Who does what

**Now, 0 members:** content 5 hrs + marketing 5 + dev 3 + admin 1 = **14 hrs/week.** That
already exceeds the available 8–12, before a single customer exists. **The first thing that
breaks is the founder, pre-launch.**

**The fix is to freeze development.** The product is live and functional. Every dev hour
before 100 members is procrastination in a hi-vis vest. **Cap dev at 1 hr/week for bugs**,
except the eight pre-launch gates.

**At 100 members:** ~15–16 hrs/week. Just survivable.
**At 500 members:** support 10–12 hrs, merch 4–6 hrs, **~30 hrs/week. Not survivable with a
day job.**

**Outsource in this order:** merch fulfilment first (sharpest cliff, physical, can't be
deferred) at **40–60 parcels/month**; support triage second at ~150 members (a VA who tags
and applies canned responses, escalating anything medical or refund-related). **Never
outsource the trial writing** — it is the product and the voice.

## 8.2 Support

**Don't buy a helpdesk.** One shared inbox with labels plus the **Stripe customer portal**,
which removes ~60% of billing contacts before they become tickets. Under £10/month. Aliases:
`office@`, `kit@`, and **`medical@` which must trigger a phone alert, not an email.**

| Type | Target | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Injury report** | **4 hours** | Same day, always |
| Billing | 24 h | 48 h |
| Stuck / can't progress | 24 h | 48 h |
| Refund | 48 h, approve by default | 5 days |
| Merch missing | 48 h acknowledge | 14 days to resolve |

**Billing.** Card declined → Smart Retries + dunning, 3 attempts over 14 days, then downgrade
to Entry-Pass state. **Freeze progress, never delete it** — frozen progress is your best
win-back asset. Double charge → refund immediately, no investigation. "I can't cancel" →
cancel it for them same day; fighting a cancellation invites a chargeback, and a lost dispute
costs **£15, non-refunded**.

**Stuck.** Three different failure modes: an app bug (your admin panel needs **force-clear
and revert-clear** — without them every bug is a refund); genuinely cannot complete the task
(**every trial needs an `ADAPTED STANDARD` line** — an accessibility requirement and a
liability shield); or lost momentum (automated nudge at 10 and 21 days idle, in system voice
— *"Bib 0043. No movement, 21 days. Checkpoint 12 remains open."*).

**Refunds — publish the rule and follow it mechanically.** Full refund on the £10 within 14
days if fewer than 2 trials cleared. Circuit Pass cancellable any time; current month
refunded on request within 7 days. Approve near-automatically: a £10 refund is cheaper than a
£15 dispute you might lose.

### Injury reports — the highest-consequence process
1. **Acknowledge within 4 hours.** No admission of liability. **No medical advice** — you aren't qualified and it destroys your insurance position. Direct to NHS 111, GP, or 999.
2. **Automatically freeze progression and pause billing** (`MEDICAL HOLD`). Continuing to charge an injured member turns an injury into a claim. Build it as an automatic consequence, not a manual step.
3. **Record it:** date, trial number, the exact briefing text at the time, their account.
4. **Review the trial.** Two reports on one trial and it comes down immediately.
5. **Notify your insurer.** Most policies require prompt notification of *any circumstance that might give rise to a claim*; late notification can void cover.
6. **Version every trial and never silently edit one.** If a trial changes after an incident, the original must remain retrievable. You will not be able to reconstruct it later.

**Merch not arrived:** free reship once at day 14, no proof required. Above 5% reship rate,
move to tracked postage.

## 8.3 Content pipeline — the biggest operational risk

**All 57 are placeholders. This is larger than anything in marketing.**

The constraint is not the median member, it's the **fastest**. Uncapped, a motivated member
clears 5–7/week and finishes in 8–11 weeks. **You would run out in under two months and the
business would publicly break.**

The cooldowns in §4.5 fix this, and fortunately the right operational answer is also the
right brand and safety answer.

| | Leader's pace | Your writing pace |
|---|---|---|
| 48h cooldown | ~15/month | 3/week ≈ 13/month |
| 72h cooldown | ~10/month | 3/week ≈ 13/month |

**With only 5 written at launch, the leader catches you in roughly ten days.**

> **Buffer policy: maintain a 30-day lead over the fastest live member at all times.**
> Put "trials written vs leader's position" on the admin panel as a single number. If the gap
> drops below 15, stop all marketing and write until it's back above 25.

**Pre-launch: 20 minimum, 30 strongly preferred.** Sustained: 3/week plus one monthly 4-hour
batch. **A fixed template is what makes ~1 hour per trial achievable** — without one, each
trial is a blank page and takes three.

**Use timed drops for 45–57** on fixed dates (the 5th and 7th suits the Primes motif). Turns
a content ceiling into a feature and removes the tail risk.

## 8.4 Merch fulfilment

**The design decision that halves the work:** make milestones 15, 30 and 45 fit a **Royal
Mail Large Letter** (under 2.5cm) — woven patch, enamel pin, numbered card, sew-on bib panel.
Only 57 is a parcel. Large Letter is far cheaper than the **£4.39 small-parcel floor** and
goes in a post box rather than requiring a Post Office trip.

| Milestone | Item | Target all-in |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | Woven patch + numbered card | £4 |
| 30 | Woven patch | £4 |
| 45 | Enamel pin / bib panel | £4 |
| 57 | Tee or plate, tracked | £16 |
| | **Per completer** | **~£28** |

*Estimates at qty 100 — get real quotes before committing.* **Milestone 15 must be the
cheapest**, because most who receive one will churn before 30.

**Workflow:** app fires on clear → `PENDING_ADDRESS` → **address collected at the milestone,
never at signup** (stale data you don't need; purge at dispatch + 90 days) → **pack once a
week in a fixed slot**, never ad hoc → pre-printed mailers with **the bib number written by
hand** (the handwriting is the emotional payload and what makes it photographable) → Click &
Drop, 2nd class → tracking only on 45 and 57.

**~4–6 min per parcel** at a batch of 10, plus 20 min fixed setup. Pain threshold ~30–40/week;
hard ceiling ~60/week alongside a day job. **Hand off at 40–60/month, before it hurts.**

## 8.5 The single-operator problem

**Survives a month unattended:** payments, gating, signup, leaderboard, transactional email,
hosting. **Breaks:** content runs out for leaders (*entirely mitigated by the buffer policy —
the 30-day buffer IS the absence policy*); merch stops; support goes silent; and **an injury
report goes unanswered for four weeks, which is the one that is genuinely dangerous.**

**Build "away mode" before launch — 4–6 hours.** One `RACE OFFICE CLOSED` flag that shows a
site banner with a return date, sets the autoresponder, **pauses milestone address emails** so
nobody is promised a parcel that isn't coming, and optionally extends cooldowns.

**The injury path must work without you.** Any submission to `medical@` or an in-app REPORT
INJURY button automatically sets `MEDICAL HOLD`, pauses billing, and auto-replies with the
safety instruction. Then a human follows up. **Name one backup person with inbox access.**

**Two more:** a self-serve **"pause my membership for a month"** button (reduces churn and
refunds), and a **dead-man's switch shipped as a cron — if no new trial is published for 60
days, Circuit Pass billing pauses automatically for everyone.** That protects members from
paying for a dead product and you from chargebacks. It's the ethical floor for taking
recurring money for content that doesn't exist yet.

**The runbook — five pages max.** Refunds in Stripe · force-clear and revert-clear · dispatch
end to end · the injury protocol verbatim · all logins in a password manager with
**emergency access configured**.

**One legal exposure to close now:** *"a permanent bib number"* is a perpetual commitment
sold for £10. Bound it in the terms — *permanent for as long as the service operates* — or it
becomes an open-ended liability the day you want to stop.
