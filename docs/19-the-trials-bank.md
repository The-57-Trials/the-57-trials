# 19. The Trials Bank — filtered

> Rob's 240-item bank of single-sitting trials, filtered against the safety exclusions
> (4.1) and the brand (12). 1 Sep 2026.
>
> **This is a reserve pool, not a running order.** Its job is to fill the ~30 non-physical
> slots in the 57, and to give any multi-day trial a 24-hour substitute.

## 19.1 Cut on safety — not negotiable, not a taste call

The global exclusions in 4.1 were written once and are marked *never added later*. They remove
most of the bank's Physical section outright, and a few others:

| Cut | Excluded because |
|---|---|
| Cold Plunge · The Contrast Trial (sauna/plunge) | Deliberate cold or heat exposure |
| Breath Hold Max | Breath-holding — named explicitly |
| Cliff Jump / Water Trial · Swim Trial | Submersion, open water, heights |
| Height Trial | Heights |
| Dead Hang Max · Plank Max · Max Rep Trial · VO2 Sprint · Balance Trial | Maximal / to-failure work |
| The Mile Under · The Century Ride · The Long Walk (30km) | Timed distance targets |
| Fasted Walk · The One-Day Fast | Fasting |
| The 80% Plate · One-Meal Discipline · Sugar-Free Day | Restrictive-eating instructions — see below |
| Barefoot Mile · Fire Trial | Injury risk, unsupervised, no gain |

**Two things worth noticing.**

Almost the entire Physical section is built on maximal effort, exposure, or a time to beat —
exactly what the product forbids. That is not a flaw in the bank. It is what "physical
challenge" normally means, and it is precisely the thing this brand deliberately is not.

> **Action: add restrictive eating to the global exclusions in 4.1.** It is the one real gap
> the bank exposed. An unsupervised product must never instruct people to eat less, and three
> items here do.

## 19.2 Cut on brand

The register is austere, private, self-attested and anti-spectacle. It refused
live-streaming (17.1) and ranked speed (D8). These do not survive that:

- **Performance for strangers** — Karaoke, The Spotlight Trial, Freestyle, Improv, The Public Failure. Influencer-challenge energy; the opposite of a private record.
- **Filmed** — The Unscripted Interview (on camera), The 12-Hour Film. The product already decided it does not ask members to broadcast.
- **Imposing on strangers** — The Rejection Sprint, Cold Approach, The Object Swap. "Cold approach" also carries a connotation this brand cannot afford to stand near.
- **Novelty for its own sake** — Coin Flip Day, Chaos Trial, Random Act Roulette, The 24-Hour Character, The Alter Ego Trial. A register does not do party games.
- **Hustle** — The £50 Sprint.
- **Irreversible on a whim** — The Non-Attachment Trial (give away ten things you are attached to). No trial should manufacture regret that cannot be undone. *Trial 53 is the one deliberate exception, and it is earned over five months.*
- **Duplicates something better** — The Time Capsule, The Legacy Letter. **Protect Trial 01.** The sealed letter is the product's signature; a second sealed letter dilutes it.
- **Too hard or too niche** — Blindfold Chess, The Deck Trial, One-Day Build, Song From Scratch.

**One rename required.** *The Witness Trial* (observing your own thoughts) collides with
witnessed trials (doc 17), which is load-bearing vocabulary. Call it **THE OBSERVER**.

## 19.4 The ten that are better than what they would replace

1. **THE UNSEEN EFFORT** — do something significant, take no credit, tell nobody, all day. **This is the entire thesis of the product written as one day's instruction.** *Your record only ever means something to you.* It should be in the 57.
2. **THE MORTALITY SIT** — thirty minutes writing your own eulogy, honestly. The heaviest thing in the bank. It belongs late; it is the natural companion to Trial 53, and it needs the MARK signpost footer.
3. **NUMBERS FAST** — a full day checking no metric about yourself. Steps, likes, weight, balance. From a product that deleted its own leaderboard, this is close to a mission statement.
4. **THE PROMISE LEDGER** — ten small promises written in the morning, every one kept by night. It is the register, in one day, at the scale of a person.
5. **THE FEEDBACK ASK** — ask three people for honest critical feedback, and only listen. Genuinely hard, entirely unverifiable, and nobody would fake it because there is nothing to win.
6. **THE FIRST AID BASICS TRIAL** — a product whose whole architecture is safety should teach it. Put it in the low 40s, before the RED chapter sends a member out alone for three hours.
7. **THE NAVIGATION TRIAL** — unfamiliar ground on a paper map, no GPS. Rehearses the RED chapter's route planning for real, and is the strongest CRAFT trial in the bank.
8. **FAMILY HISTORY TRIAL** — interview an older relative for thirty minutes and record it. The only trial here that produces something a member will still have in twenty years. It also feeds the book.
9. **THE FORGIVENESS LETTER** — a letter, one sitting, which the Library already knows how to hold. Reuses the letter UI built for Trial 01 at no extra cost.
10. **SOLO OUTING TRIAL** — go somewhere alone for the first time. A restaurant, an event, a trip. Quietly one of the hardest things on the list for the people this product is actually for.

## 19.5 What the bank does not solve

**MOVE.** Six survivors against roughly thirty slots. Every other physical item is a maximal
effort, an exposure, or a time to beat, and the exclusions remove all three.

Those ~30 physical trials still have to be written from scratch, in the FIRST GROUND / THE
STACK / THE CARRY idiom: **a completed quantity at a controlled effort, with a scale that
always clears.** That is still the largest single body of writing left in the project, and the
bank does not shorten it.

What the bank does do is remove all doubt about the other thirty.
